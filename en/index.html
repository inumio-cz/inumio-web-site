Products

iFlow

iFlow is a ready-made core system for asset management companies and securities brokers. It fully automates the entire investment transaction lifecycle, from the client’s order to final settlement.

The system is ready for implementation at your company. We will discuss the implementation scope and connections to your systems based on your individual needs.

OrderChecksSettlement
  • Order management
  • Static data management
  • Client asset records
  • Corporate actions
  • Incoming and outgoing payments
More about iFlowHide details
  • Order management: purchases, sales, switches, transfers and reinvestments in a single flow – from order entry and checks through to settlement.
  • Static data management: funds and their classes, other instruments, fees, calendars and code lists in one place. A change applies wherever the data is used.
  • Client asset records: each client’s asset accounts, positions and cash, including valuation and holdings as of a chosen date.
  • Corporate actions: process them in the system and see their impact reflected in your clients’ holdings.
  • Incoming and outgoing payments: match incoming payments to clients and their orders, and prepare outgoing payments for sending.

iHub — connect your systems and processes

Connect your core investment system, business applications, banks and other partners. iHub coordinates data exchange and process steps between them. Your operations team can see what has happened and where action may be needed.

More about iHubHide details
  • A shared interface between apps and connected systems.
  • Coordination of multi-step processes, such as creating a client and an agreement.
  • Visibility into messages, errors and reprocessing options.
  • Data format conversion when connecting partners.

We design specific connections and operating arrangements around your environment. We can explore the technical details in a demonstration.

Talk about iHub →

iBase — product data in one place

Manage products, prices, regulatory documents and commercial terms in one place. iBase feeds them into your apps and helps you keep track of what you offer, to whom and on which terms.

More about iBaseHide details
  • A shared catalogue with availability settings by service, channel or client segment.
  • Collection and standardisation of data and documents from connected sources.
  • Alerts for incomplete data and unusual price changes.
  • A history of product parameters and documents.

We select data sources and their permitted use for each implementation. The catalogue can connect to your own applications.

Talk about iBase →

iTrend — portfolio calculations for your apps

How has a portfolio performed, and how might it develop? iTrend calculates historical value, performance and modelled scenarios. It takes contributions, withdrawals and fees into account and returns the results to your application.

More about iTrendHide details
  • Historical valuation and performance at portfolio, service and product level.
  • Regular and one-off cash flows, fees and dividends.
  • Optimistic, central and pessimistic modelled scenarios with information about estimate uncertainty.
  • Outputs for client applications and modelling within the agreed scope.

Scenarios illustrate possible outcomes based on assumptions; they do not promise returns.
